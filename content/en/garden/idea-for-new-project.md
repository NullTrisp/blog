**Concept:** A personal AI assistant that helps track expenses, categorize income, and generate financial reports from various sources (bank statements, receipts, Google Drive documents).

**Key Features:**
*   Automated data extraction from PDFs/CSVs.
*   Categorization based on user-defined rules.
*   Monthly/yearly report generation.
*   Integration with Google Drive for document access.

**Initial Thoughts:**
*   Need OCR for receipts.
*   NLP for categorizing transactions.
*   Secure storage for financial data.
