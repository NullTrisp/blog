**Artist:** Radiohead
**Album:** OK Computer
**Year:** 1997

**Why I love it:**
A masterpiece of alternative rock, "OK Computer" perfectly captures the anxieties of modern life and technology at the turn of the millennium. Its haunting melodies, intricate arrangements, and profound lyrics make it a timeless classic.

**Standout Tracks:**

- "Paranoid Android"
- "Karma Police"
- "No Surprises"
